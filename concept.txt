Go out with friends: Increase social life, happiness gain depends on *friendship health*. money, time, hunger, rest spend/gain depends on event

Hunger : *Eat Outside* with money, or spend *time* to *cook*. Eat outside depending on place, can cause *food poisoning* which spends time. often food poisoning lessens chance.
Money : Set amount
Hygiene : spend *time* to *shower*
